# First trip

The onboarding checklist, in order. Your AI works through this with you —
not for you.

- [ ] GitHub established — an account your person controls, this repo
      created from the template, named `<initial>ao`, private.
- [ ] The agent reads the commons — the guide, the constitution, and
      CONTRIBUTING at https://github.com/Scottpedia0/_ao — and keeps a
      durable pointer to the constitution in its first-loaded context file.
- [ ] The question-set conversation — the agent asks its human what this AO
      is for: what work matters, what is off-limits, what "done" looks like,
      who else is in the system. Answers land in contracts/, in the human's
      own words.
- [ ] The contracts take first shape — operating contract, agent roster,
      boundaries. Rough is fine; written is the point.
