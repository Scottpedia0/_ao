# Operating contract

How this AO runs: who decides, what the agents may do without asking, and what always comes back to the human. Written by the human and their AI together; revised whenever reality disagrees with it.

Includes the identity policy — whether the AI writes as itself, as you, or decides situationally, and whether batches of outbound work get a check-in first or run free within the policy. Decided on day one; revisable like everything here.
