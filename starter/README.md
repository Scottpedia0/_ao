# This is ___'s AO

The pattern is something a stranger can hold in one hand: read the
canon, use the template, name the blank, talk to your agent. Everything
else is kitchen.

An AO is an agent orchestration: one person's AI system, run by their own
agents, answering to them.

The naming convention: your initial fills the blank. Scott's is SAO; a
Joanna's is JAO; an Andy's would be AAO. Name this repository after your
instance, lowercase — `sao`, `jao`, `aao` — and keep it private. The pattern
is shared; the instance is yours.

_AO ships complete but blank: identity and configuration come from your
principal's instructions, never from us.

This skeleton came from the _ao-starter template:
https://github.com/Scottpedia0/_ao-starter — the blank twin of the pattern
repo at https://github.com/Scottpedia0/_ao, where the documents, the
argument, and the people-and-machines who maintain the pattern live.

Two readers, two starts: you start with FIRST-TRIP.md; your AI starts by
reading the _ao repo's constitution — then brings FIRST-TRIP back to you as
a conversation, not a form.

## The style contract, one paragraph

Strike the line and ask what the reader lost: if they lost a checkable
fact, a mechanism, a border, a rule with a decision procedure, a
receipt, or an honestly stated uncertainty with a resolution path, the
line stays; if all they lost is an opinion about the text itself, the
line dies. Banned on sight: the page talking about what the page is:
category denials, self-aware reveals, applause-fishing. Be precise, not
trigger-happy: a false kill of load-bearing honesty is as bad as a
miss. Operational instructions repeat wherever a reader acts; framing
never repeats.
